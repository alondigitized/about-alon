Retail Intelligence Pulse

Sunday, August 09 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

What newly matters now

PUMA and Target are making two sides of the same move. PUMA is deliberately shrinking undesirable wholesale, especially in North America and Europe, while keeping e-commerce growing and lifting DTC mix. Target is expanding its curated marketplace and explicitly trying to surface that assortment inside AI-powered search and discovery paths. The strategic signal is clean: growth is shifting away from blunt distribution expansion and toward tighter channel control, curated assortment and algorithmic discovery.

Executive takeaway: treat wholesale pruning, marketplace participation and search/discovery visibility as one integrated distribution strategy. If those teams are operating separately, you are probably leaking margin or demand.

PUMA is showing what a deliberate wholesale reset actually looks like in footwear

PUMA said on July 31, 2026 that second-quarter currency-adjusted sales fell 9.4% to €1.69 billion, but gross margin rose about 180 basis points to 48.0%, inventories fell 15.3% to €1.82 billion and free cash flow rose to €328.8 million from €94.9 million a year earlier.

The company said wholesale declined 14.0% currency-adjusted to €1.09 billion because it continued reducing undesirable wholesale business, particularly in North America and Europe, versus a prior-year quarter that still included a high share of mass-merchant business.

PUMA said DTC rose 0.4% currency-adjusted to €595.8 million, with e-commerce up 1.8% despite lower promotions, helped by additional marketplaces in APAC and growth in Greater China. DTC mix increased to 35.2% from 32.1% a year earlier.

PUMA Q2 2026 results release

This is not a generic weak quarter. It is a concrete example of a footwear brand accepting near-term revenue pain to clean up distribution and inventory economics. The interesting part is not the sales decline; it is that PUMA kept e-commerce positive with lower promotions while pruning low-quality wholesale. That suggests healthier future demand capture matters more than defending every door.

Pressure-test your own North America distribution: which accounts, marketplaces or off-price pathways are creating volume but weakening pricing power, forecast quality or brand heat? If you cannot name them precisely, your reset is probably not specific enough.

Target is pushing curated marketplace inventory into AI-powered discovery, not just adding long-tail assortment

Target said on July 1, 2026 that it is expanding Target Plus with dozens of new hand-picked brands, including Clarks, Forever 21 and JanSport, to extend its digital assortment while keeping the marketplace invite-only and curated.

Target Chief Digital and Revenue Officer Sarah Travis said Target Plus helps the retailer respond faster to demand shifts, gives brands a way to reach Target shoppers, and now accounts for more than half of Target's K-beauty assortment offering.

Target also said discovery work now includes improving on-platform search, integrating Target Plus more fully into promotions and showing marketplace items more clearly in AI-powered search and other digital discovery experiences through partners including OpenAI, Google and Microsoft.

Target Plus growth and discovery article

That matters because it reframes marketplace strategy. The real game is no longer just adding third-party assortment to fill gaps. It is making curated external assortment discoverable wherever shoppers start their journey, including AI interfaces that may sit outside the retailer's owned site. For footwear brands, that changes the value of being present inside curated retailer marketplaces: the shelf is becoming an algorithmic surface, not just a category page.

Ask two hard questions now: which partners are most likely to surface your products inside AI-assisted shopping journeys, and do your product content, pricing and availability make you look premium or generic once an agent intermediates the search?

Evidence note: this Target item is company-provided strategy communication, so it is strong evidence of intent, operating direction and named partner references, but not proof yet of conversion, margin or sustained traffic impact.
