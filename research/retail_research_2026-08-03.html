Retail Intelligence Pulse

Monday, August 03 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

What newly matters now

Three signals from the past two weeks point in the same direction. Deckers just showed HOKA can still grow while keeping both wholesale and direct channels healthy. PUMA elevated e-commerce into a dedicated executive role after splitting retail and digital inside DTC. Target is explicitly wiring curated marketplace inventory into AI-driven discovery and account-linked checkout. The common message: the next retail edge is not just traffic or doors. It is owning how assortment, channel mix and machine-mediated discovery work together.

Executive takeaway: treat AI discovery, marketplace curation and DTC operating ownership as one commercial system, not separate teams.

Deckers is still proving that premium footwear can scale without breaking channel economics

Deckers said on July 23 that first-quarter fiscal 2027 net sales increased 5.7% to $1.02 billion, its first time above $1 billion in a first quarter.

HOKA net sales increased 7.7% to $703.5 million, while wholesale net sales increased 2.2% to $666.7 million and direct-to-consumer net sales increased 13.0% to $352.8 million.

Deckers also said DTC comparable net sales increased 6.8% and it raised full-year fiscal 2027 diluted EPS guidance to a range of $7.35 to $7.50.

Deckers fiscal Q1 2027 results

For footwear leaders, the useful signal is not just that HOKA is still growing. It is that Deckers is getting growth from both wholesale and DTC at the same time while still lifting guidance. That suggests disciplined channel segmentation can expand reach without automatically dumping margin or brand control.

Pressure-test where your own channel mix is additive versus cannibalistic. If wholesale growth is not improving inventory turns, data capture or premium presentation, it is probably lower quality than it looks.

PUMA is turning e-commerce into a distinct management priority, not a sub-function of DTC

PUMA said on July 22 that it appointed Dusan Hamlin to the newly created role of Vice President E-Commerce, reporting to Chief Commercial Officer Matthias Bäumer.

PUMA said Hamlin will drive the global strategy and commercial direction of its digital and e-commerce businesses.

The company also said the appointment follows its late-2025 decision to split direct-to-consumer into two dedicated areas: Global Retail and Global E-Commerce.

PUMA e-commerce leadership announcement

This is an org-design signal. PUMA is effectively saying e-commerce now merits dedicated leadership, platform capability building and a separate operating model rather than being managed as one slice of a broader store-led DTC function. That is especially relevant as social commerce and AI-assisted commerce start changing how shoppers discover and convert.

Review whether digital commerce inside your organization has true operating ownership or is still structurally subordinate to broader retail agendas. If the latter, speed and accountability will lag as discovery shifts toward AI and social surfaces.

Target is pushing curated marketplace assortment into AI-native shopping flows

Target said on June 18 that it is the first mass retailer with shopping experiences available across Google Search and Gemini, Microsoft Copilot and OpenAI's ChatGPT, including browsing, basket building and links to Target Circle loyalty accounts.

Target also said AI-driven traffic to its retail site increased 2,000% in the first quarter versus last year, compared with nearly 400% growth for retail sites overall.

On July 1, Target said it is expanding Target Plus with brands including Clarks, Forever 21, JanSport, Serta, JLab and Hisense, and that Target Plus items will show up more clearly in AI-powered search and other digital discovery experiences through partners including OpenAI, Google and Microsoft.

Target is treating marketplace expansion and AI discovery as linked moves. That matters because curated third-party assortment becomes more valuable when it can surface inside conversational shopping environments without forcing the retailer to own all inventory. For footwear and apparel leaders, this is a live example of how marketplace breadth and loyalty-linked checkout can reinforce each other.

Re-rank marketplace partners and owned-site priorities around AI discoverability: structured product data, strong imagery, clean availability and loyalty hooks now matter earlier in the funnel.

Evidence notes

All hard facts above come from company materials published between June 18 and July 23, 2026. The strategic read is interpretive. Target's AI traffic and shopping-flow claims are company-reported and not broken out into disclosed revenue contribution, so the commercial impact should be treated as directional rather than fully proven.
