Retail Intelligence Pulse

Friday, October 30 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

Digital Commerce

Nike announced a new AI‑driven product recommendation engine on its e‑commerce platform, projected to lift conversion rates by 8–12% (Nike Press Release, Oct 27, 2026).

Higher conversion rates will compress CAC and improve margin in direct-to-consumer channels.

Adopt similar AI personalization in your own website’s recommendation layer to capture comparable lift.

Marketplaces

Adidas signed a multi‑year partnership with Amazon to list its flagship line on the Amazon Marketplace, citing access to 200M monthly shoppers (Adidas Investor Relations, Oct 28, 2026).

This move broadens Adidas’ reach and creates inventory pressure, requiring tighter fulfillment sync.

Review your own marketplace agreements for fulfillment integration gaps.

Loyalty & CRM

Walmart rolled out a new omni‑channel loyalty program integrating Walmart+ with its digital and physical stores, expected to increase repeat visits by 15% (Walmart Corporate Blog, Oct 29, 2026).

Enhanced loyalty will drive cross‑channel shopping behavior and data richness.

Consider aligning your loyalty incentives with both online and offline touchpoints.

Supply Chain Technology

New Balance announced a real‑time inventory visibility platform with SAP Cloud Platform to sync with Amazon and Walmart (New Balance Press Release, Oct 26, 2026).

Real‑time inventory reduces stockouts and overstock, improving customer satisfaction.

Evaluate your current ERP integration with major marketplaces.
