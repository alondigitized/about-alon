Retail Intelligence Pulse

Tuesday, July 28 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

What newly matters now

Three recent signals now line up cleanly. Deckers just showed that faster DTC growth can come with lower inventory and a higher earnings outlook. PUMA just created a dedicated vice president role for e-commerce after already splitting retail and e-commerce inside DTC. Target keeps proving that curated marketplace expansion is not just assortment inflation; it is part of a more controlled digital discovery model. Different business models, same strategic lesson: digital commerce leadership is consolidating around tighter control of channel economics, digital shelf curation and explicit ownership of the online customer journey.

Executive takeaway: do not treat DTC, marketplaces and e-commerce org design as separate workstreams. The winners are stitching them into one operating model.

Deckers just gave the clearest current proof that DTC quality matters more than DTC purity

Deckers reported on July 23 that first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue rose 5.7% to $1.02 billion, while direct-to-consumer net sales increased 13.0% to $352.8 million and wholesale net sales increased 2.2% to $670.0 million.

The company said inventories fell to $807.6 million from $849.4 million a year earlier.

Deckers also raised full-year diluted EPS guidance to $7.35-$7.50, while Reuters noted the earnings beat and guidance lift were supported by demand for HOKA and UGG plus growth in the higher-margin DTC business.

Deckers Brands Q1 FY2027 results · Reuters on Deckers results

This is not a generic growth story. It is evidence that premium footwear can still grow owned channels faster than wholesale without losing inventory discipline. That is strategically stronger than the old DTC narrative, which often chased mix shift without proving better operating health.

Use DTC as a margin-quality and inventory-control instrument, not as a vanity share target. If digital growth is not improving stock discipline and forecast confidence, it is weaker than it looks.

PUMA is formalizing e-commerce as a top-level operating capability

PUMA announced on July 22 that it appointed Dusan Hamlin to the newly created role of Vice President E-Commerce, reporting to Chief Commercial Officer Matthias Bäumer.

The company said Hamlin will drive the global strategy and commercial direction of PUMA's digital and e-commerce businesses.

PUMA explicitly linked the appointment to its late-2025 decision to split direct-to-consumer into two dedicated areas: Global Retail and Global E-Commerce.

PUMA appoints Dusan Hamlin as Vice President E-Commerce

This is an org-design tell. PUMA is saying e-commerce is no longer just one channel inside DTC; it now deserves distinct leadership, platform capability and commercial accountability. For footwear brands still blending retail, digital trading, CRM and marketplace management into one blurry structure, that is a warning.

Audit whether e-commerce has real decision rights in your model. If digital trading, site experience, app, marketplaces and lifecycle marketing are fragmented across different leaders, speed will leak at exactly the point where competitors are centralizing it.

Target keeps showing what curated marketplace expansion looks like when a retailer wants control, not chaos

Target said on July 1 that it is adding dozens of new Target Plus brands this summer, including Clarks, Forever 21, JanSport, JLab and others, while keeping the marketplace invite-only.

Chief Digital and Revenue Officer Sarah Travis said the model is designed to support “curation at scale,” and that Target Plus items are being surfaced more clearly in AI-powered search and discovery experiences through partnerships with OpenAI, Google and Microsoft.

On Target's May 20 earnings call, management said Target Plus posted nearly 60% growth in first-quarter GMV.

Target Plus growth update · Target Q1 2026 earnings call transcript

Target is not expanding marketplace assortment the Amazon way. It is using marketplace capacity as a curated extension of merchandising authority, then making that assortment more visible inside AI-mediated discovery. For brands, that means acceptance into curated retailer marketplaces increasingly doubles as admission into machine-readable shopping environments.

Treat curated marketplace readiness as part of digital shelf strategy. Brand content, pricing integrity and assortment fitness now affect not only on-site search but whether you surface cleanly in AI-assisted discovery layers.
