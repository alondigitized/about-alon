Retail Intelligence Pulse

Friday, August 14 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

What newly matters now

The market signal is getting cleaner. Premium footwear names that still have pricing power are converting it into stronger direct-to-consumer growth, while the large retailers are building tighter control over how discovery happens before the click. That combination matters more than generic e-commerce growth: brands need both healthier owned demand and better placement inside curated, AI-mediated retail environments.

Executive takeaway: treat DTC productivity and third-party discovery control as one agenda. If you win only one side, you are leaving margin or demand on the table.

Crocs and Deckers both point to the same premium-footwear pattern: cleaner demand, better DTC, less dependence on wholesale volume

Crocs said on July 30 that second-quarter 2026 consolidated revenue rose 2.6% to $1.179 billion, while DTC revenue grew 12.0% and wholesale fell 7.2%.

In the same release, the Crocs brand surpassed $1 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, with brand DTC revenue up 12.9% to $559 million and wholesale down 5.0% to $441 million. Crocs also raised full-year 2026 revenue and EPS guidance.

Crocs Q2 2026 results

Deckers said on July 23 that first-quarter fiscal 2027 net sales rose 5.7% to $1.02 billion, with HOKA up 7.7% to $703.5 million. Deckers' DTC sales rose 13.0% to $352.8 million while wholesale rose 2.2%.

Deckers also raised full-year fiscal 2027 EPS guidance to $7.35-$7.50.

Deckers Q1 FY2027 results

These are not just good quarters. They show that strong footwear brands are still finding profitable growth through their own channels even when wholesale is either shrinking or growing slower. That is strategically important because it preserves pricing authority, first-party customer signal and launch control.

Pressure-test which parts of your assortment genuinely earn DTC emphasis. The winners are using owned channels to concentrate premium demand, not to absorb whatever volume wholesale cannot take.

Target is trying to own the AI shopping shelf before it becomes the default retail homepage

Target said on June 18 that AI-driven traffic to Target rose 2,000% in the first quarter versus last year and that it had shopping experiences live across Google Search and AI Mode, the Gemini app, Microsoft Copilot and OpenAI's ChatGPT.

Target also said more of the shopping journey can happen directly in conversational interfaces, including browsing, building multi-item baskets and linking to Target Circle loyalty accounts.

Target conversational AI fact sheet

On July 1, Target said Target Plus is continuing to add hand-picked brands and emphasized that the invite-only marketplace is designed to extend assortment while preserving curation. Target also said it wants marketplace items to show up more clearly in AI-powered search through work with OpenAI, Google and Microsoft.

Target Plus update

Target is making a deliberate bet that marketplace expansion only works if discovery stays curated. That is a meaningful contrast to open marketplace scale models. For brands, this raises the value of being machine-readable and merchant-worthy at the same time.

Audit product content, inventory availability, pricing consistency and loyalty hooks as if AI surfaces are now a major merchandising channel. In practice, they are becoming one.

Walmart's latest operating data shows the other side of the control stack: fast fulfillment plus higher-margin platform services

Walmart said on May 21 that global eCommerce grew 26% in Q1 FY27. Walmart U.S. eCommerce also grew 26%, and more than 36% of store-fulfilled delivery orders were delivered in under three hours.

Walmart also said global advertising grew 37%, Walmart Connect U.S. grew 44% excluding VIZIO, and growth in advertising, membership and marketplace is improving the company's mix.

Walmart Q1 FY27 results

Walmart's signal is that retail advantage is no longer just traffic plus inventory. It is traffic plus fulfillment speed plus monetizable platform services. That makes the retailer harder to bypass and changes the economics for every branded supplier inside the ecosystem.

If your team still manages marketplace, retail media and fulfillment economics as separate workstreams, that structure is now behind the market.
