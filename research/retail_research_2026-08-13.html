Retail Intelligence Pulse

Thursday, August 13 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

What newly matters now

The sharpest shift is not another generic AI announcement. It is the closing of the loop between discovery, conversion and monetization. Amazon is proving that AI shopping can already lift order economics and ad performance inside a scaled retail system. At the same time, Nike and adidas are showing two different realities in footwear: Nike is still repairing digital traffic and marketplace health, while adidas is converting stronger demand into double-digit DTC growth without giving up full-price discipline. The implication is blunt: traffic quality and channel control now matter more than just having a bigger digital business.

Executive takeaway: treat AI discovery, owned-channel traffic quality and full-price marketplace discipline as one operating system, not three separate workstreams.

Amazon is building the clearest current model for AI commerce that actually monetizes

Amazon said on July 30, 2026 that it combined Rufus and Alexa+ into Alexa for Shopping, an agentic shopping assistant offering personalized recommendations, product comparisons, price history, Price Alerts and Auto-Buy.

In the same release, Amazon said active users of these shopping experiences were close to doubling and interactions were up more than 5x year over year in Q2.

Amazon also said advertisers using Ads Agent see 8% lower cost per impression and 6% lower cost per acquisition, while a separate company summary said Amazon Ads revenue reached $19.8 billion in Q2, up 26% year over year.

Amazon Q2 2026 results · Amazon Ads Q2 commentary

This is what matters strategically: Amazon is no longer just adding an AI layer to search. It is connecting conversational discovery, ad targeting, checkout automation and fulfillment inside one flywheel. That makes Amazon harder to disintermediate and raises the bar for every brand trying to win traffic outside Amazon.

Benchmark against Amazon’s closed loop. Ask whether your organization can measure AI-assisted discovery, product consideration, conversion and media yield as one funnel rather than separate commerce and marketing dashboards.

Nike’s latest results still show a traffic problem, not just a product problem

Nike reported on June 30, 2026 that fourth-quarter NIKE Direct revenues fell 7% to $4.1 billion, with NIKE Brand Digital down 12% and NIKE-owned stores down 7%.

Nike’s CFO said the company is "managing our product portfolio and investing in marketplace elevation" while adjusting operating costs for efficiency.

Nike fiscal 2026 Q4 results

Nike’s wording matters more than the quarterly decline alone. When management emphasizes marketplace elevation while digital traffic is falling, the issue is broader than SKU misses. It suggests the brand is still trying to rebuild where and how consumers discover product, how cleanly inventory flows through channels, and which surfaces deserve full-price exposure.

If traffic is soft, do not default to blaming creative or media efficiency. Pressure-test whether marketplace architecture, assortment clarity and search/discovery quality are depressing conversion before the consumer even sees the hero product.

adidas is the cleaner counterexample: stronger DTC growth while holding the line on full price

adidas said on April 29, 2026 that all markets posted double-digit DTC growth in Q1 2026.

The company also said it sustained a full-price sales focus across DTC, leading to higher gross margins in both e-commerce and own retail.

At the same time, adidas said it maintained a conservative wholesale sell-in approach, especially in Europe and North America, given promotional intensity in the marketplace.

adidas Q1 2026 results

adidas is showing the more durable formula: tighter wholesale discipline plus healthier owned-channel demand. That is strategically more important than headline growth because it signals better control over price integrity, inventory flow and brand presentation across channels.

Use adidas as the operating benchmark for marketplace discipline. The standard is not merely growing DTC; it is growing DTC while preserving gross margin and resisting the temptation to overfeed wholesale in a promotional market.

Cross-market proof that AI discovery is becoming commercially material

Adobe said on June 17, 2026 that AI traffic to U.S. retail sites surged 1,324% between October 2024 and May 2026.

Adobe also said on June 29, 2026 that during the June 23-26 Prime Day period, AI-referred traffic to U.S. retail sites was up 89% and converted 40% better than non-AI channels.

Adobe Brand Visibility release · Adobe Prime Day analysis

The important nuance is that AI traffic is still relatively small, but it is no longer trivial. Higher-converting AI-referred visits mean discovery economics are starting to shift before traffic volume does. Brands that wait for this channel to become large before adapting will be late.

Start treating LLM and AI-surface visibility like a real performance channel now. That means structured product data, clean availability signals, strong content attribution and measurement that isolates AI-referred traffic quality from generic organic traffic.
