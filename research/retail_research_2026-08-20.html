Retail Intelligence Pulse

Thursday, August 20 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

What newly matters now

Walmart just validated the same core pattern Target showed yesterday: large-scale retail ecosystems are still winning because marketplace breadth, loyalty income, advertising monetization and store-based fulfillment are reinforcing one another. For footwear brands, that raises the bar again. Winning distribution is no longer just shelf space or digital assortment. It is whether a product is visible, fulfillable and profitable inside retailer operating systems that increasingly decide discovery and conversion.

Executive takeaway: treat Walmart and Target this week as one signal, not two separate earnings prints. The operating model that is working right now is ecosystem retail with strong digital plumbing, not broad undifferentiated wholesale reach.

Walmart just confirmed that omnichannel flywheels are still compounding

Walmart said on Thursday, August 20, that Q2 FY2027 revenue rose 5.9% to $187.9 billion, global eCommerce sales grew 23%, global advertising grew 38%, membership fee revenue grew 17%, and the company raised its full-year outlook. Walmart now expects FY2027 net sales growth of 4.0% to 5.0% and adjusted EPS of $2.80 to $2.87.

In Walmart U.S., the presentation said comp sales were up 2.6%, eCommerce sales rose 24%, Walmart Connect ex-VIZIO grew 43%, marketplace sales grew 52%, store-fulfilled delivery grew about 43%, expedited deliveries under three hours represented about 37% of store-fulfilled orders, and Walmart+ posted record second-quarter net adds.

Walmart Q2 FY2027 presentation (PDF)

That mix matters more than headline revenue. Walmart is showing that traffic, monetization and convenience are moving together: marketplace expands choice, advertising monetizes demand, membership lowers friction, and stores keep delivery fast enough to protect conversion. That is a structurally stronger model than chasing volume with blunt markdowns.

If Walmart is a priority account, audit hero SKUs for three things immediately: local inventory accuracy, content readiness for search and recommendations, and eligibility for fast fulfillment. A product that is not fast-delivery-ready is increasingly not fully discoverable.

Target and Walmart together now make this a strategic pattern, not a one-off quarter

Target said on Wednesday, August 19, that Q2 2026 store comparable sales grew 2.7%, digital comparable sales grew 8.7%, same-day delivery grew more than 25%, non-merchandise sales grew more than 20%, and full-year net sales growth is now expected around 5%. Target said non-merchandise strength reflected Roundel, Target Circle 360 and Target Plus.

Target Q2 2026 earnings summary

Both companies also disclosed material tariff-refund benefits in the quarter. Target said it recognized $994 million in tariff refunds during Q2 2026. Walmart said tariff refunds boosted gross profit and operating income, while also noting that it used part of the benefit for price investments.

The refund effect means neither quarter should be read as pure clean-margin proof. But the strategically important signal is separate from that accounting help: both retailers still posted strong digital and ecosystem metrics at the same time. That suggests the control points in U.S. retail are hardening around membership, media, marketplace and fulfillment quality.

For footwear brands, this is the week to stop treating retail media, marketplace assortment, loyalty-funded fulfillment and wholesale account planning as separate workstreams. The retailers that are winning are operating them as one system.

Evidence notes

Today's core fact set comes primarily from Walmart's own earnings release and presentation, which are strong primary sources. The Target comparison is also from company materials, but it is one day older and is used here as context for the new Walmart signal. The biggest caveat is that tariff refunds boosted both companies' quarters, so profit strength should be separated from the underlying digital operating trends.
