Retail Intelligence Pulse

Monday, July 27 2026 · Footwear, digital commerce, marketplaces

What newly matters now

The cleanest signal in retail right now is not flashy AI messaging. It is operating control. Deckers just showed that higher-margin DTC growth can coexist with inventory discipline and a guidance raise. lululemon is scaling e-commerce fulfillment with serious automation infrastructure, not just softer process language. On is rewiring leadership so DTC, digital, retail and loyalty sit under a dedicated customer owner. Different companies, same conclusion: winning digital commerce in footwear and adjacent retail now depends on who controls the relationship, the inventory flow and the decision rights around customer experience.

Executive takeaway: treat DTC economics, fulfillment automation and customer ownership as one operating system. If they sit in separate silos, speed and margin will leak.

Deckers used DTC strength and inventory discipline to raise the quality bar

Deckers reported on July 23 that first-quarter fiscal 2027 revenue rose 5.7% to $1.02 billion, with HOKA net sales up 7.7% to $703.5 million.

Direct-to-consumer net sales increased 13.0% to $352.8 million, faster than wholesale growth of 2.2%.

Inventories were $807.6 million versus $849.4 million a year earlier, and Deckers raised full-year diluted EPS guidance to $7.35-$7.50 while keeping full-year sales guidance at $5.86-$5.91 billion.

Deckers Brands Q1 FY2027 results

This is strategically stronger than a plain earnings beat. Deckers is showing that a premium footwear portfolio can still grow DTC faster than wholesale without letting inventory balloon. That matters because a lot of brand operators still act as if channel mix upgrades and inventory control naturally conflict. Deckers just reported evidence that they do not have to.

Pressure-test whether your digital channel is improving margin quality or just shifting volume. The key operating question is whether DTC growth is arriving alongside cleaner inventory and guidance confidence.

lululemon is scaling fulfillment with hard automation, not rhetoric

lululemon said its new Brampton, Ontario distribution center was fully operational as of June 2026 and supports e-commerce fulfillment across Eastern Canada and the Eastern United States.

The facility spans more than one million square feet and includes an AutoStore system with 292,000 storage bins and 525 robots.

Chief Supply Chain Officer Ted Dagnese said the site expands fulfillment capabilities and helps the company operate with greater speed and agility; lululemon celebrated the opening on July 8.

lululemon Brampton distribution center announcement

For digital leaders, the important point is not the square footage. It is that e-commerce growth is being underwritten by physical automation density. When retailers talk about personalization, faster delivery and better omnichannel service, this is the substrate that makes those promises economically survivable.

Map the next two years of digital ambition against actual node capacity, automation depth and regional fulfillment logic. If the network cannot support faster promise times and cleaner returns economics, the front-end strategy is overstated.

On is making customer ownership a top-level leadership function

On announced on July 7 that it is splitting commercial leadership into two roles and adding Alice Delahunt as Chief Customer Officer, effective September 7.

On said Delahunt will oversee direct-to-consumer channels across digital and retail as well as the brand's focus on customer loyalty.

At the same time, Rebecca Cai was promoted to Chief Global Markets Officer, separating market leadership from customer ownership.

On leadership announcement

This is an org-design tell. On is explicitly saying that managing markets is not the same job as owning DTC experience, retail execution and loyalty. That separation should get the attention of larger footwear companies whose customer agenda is still fragmented across merchandising, e-commerce, CRM and stores.

Look at who in your model actually owns the lifetime customer relationship end to end. If loyalty, retail, digital and brand experience answer to different leaders with weak arbitration, that is a structural handicap.

Evidence notes

All factual points above come directly from company disclosures or company-published announcements dated July 7, July 8 and July 23, 2026. The strategic read-throughs are analysis, not company guidance. No weak second-hand reporting was used in this brief.
